METHODS & SAFETY

Research information.

NeuroCardioTwin is a research interface—not a clinical decision-support product.

Architecture

Separate EEG seizure, ECG arrhythmia and joint forecasting paths are designed to communicate through backend APIs. Reliability-aware fusion is a research feature, not an implemented clinical claim.

Data sources

Research may use CHB-MIT Scalp EEG, MIT-BIH Arrhythmia and MIT-BIH Polysomnographic datasets, subject to their licences and correct study protocol.

Validation

Performance must be reported from held-out, appropriately split evaluation data. Dataset shift, recording quality and label uncertainty limit generalisation.

Safety notice

Do not use this site for diagnosis, treatment, emergency triage or patient monitoring. All analysis requires expert review and independent validation.
