# Research-output safety

NeuroCardioTwin is a competition research prototype. EEG and ECG model outputs are numerical research signals, not clinical diagnoses. Before interpreting an output, verify the acquisition channel, sample count, sampling rate, signal quality, preprocessing and model version. A model probability is not a patient-level risk estimate. Do not use this system to make triage, treatment, medication or emergency decisions. Escalate potentially urgent symptoms or clinical concerns to qualified medical professionals and local emergency services.
